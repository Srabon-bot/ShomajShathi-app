## If you keep any of these

- **CC BY 4.0 / CC BY-SA 4.0** require visible attribution. Add a small
  "Photography credits" link in the footer pointing at this file.
- **CC BY-SA 4.0** is share-alike: if you distribute modified versions, the
  derivative must carry the same licence. Attribution + link is the normal
  way to satisfy it for a website.
- Photos of identifiable people used to imply endorsement are a separate
  issue. None of the above suggest endorsement, and none name a beneficiary.

## Better than all of the above

Shoot one real drive yourself — 8–10 frames on a phone, morning light, wide
and detail shots. Authentic local photography outperforms every stock library
for this brief, and it removes the licensing question entirely.
